HOW TO OPEN PENTIS ON A MAC
===========================

Pentis is a small independent game and is not (yet) registered with
Apple. Because of that, macOS will block it the first time you open it.
This is normal. You only have to do the steps below ONCE.


1. INSTALL
----------
- Unzip the download (double-click the .zip file).
- Drag the Pentis app into your Applications folder.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
- Double-click Pentis.
  macOS shows a message like "Pentis Not Opened" or "Apple could not
  verify Pentis is free of malware". Click "Done" (or "OK").

- Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
- Scroll down to the "Security" section. You will see:
      "Pentis" was blocked to protect your Mac.
  Click "Open Anyway".
- Confirm with your password (or Touch ID), then click "Open Anyway"
  once more.

Pentis now starts, and from now on it opens normally with a double-click.

On older macOS versions (Sonoma 14 or earlier) there is a shortcut:
right-click (or Control-click) Pentis, choose "Open", then click "Open"
in the message that appears.


3. IF MACOS SAYS "PENTIS IS DAMAGED AND CAN'T BE OPENED"
-------------------------------------------------------
The game is not damaged - this is macOS being strict about apps
downloaded from the internet. To fix it:

- Open the Terminal app (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
- Type the following, WITH a space at the end, but do NOT press Return yet:

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine

- Drag the Pentis app from your Applications folder into the Terminal
  window. Its location is filled in for you.
- Press Return, then close Terminal.

Now double-click Pentis again.
